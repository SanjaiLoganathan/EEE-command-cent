    How to use your learning plan

    1. Start with Day 1 and follow one lesson per day. Your course day starts when you first open this updated site.
    2. Spend 25 minutes learning the concept, 20 minutes solving problems, 10 minutes applying it, and 5 minutes recalling it without notes.
    3. On practical days, use safe low-voltage simulations or virtual tools. Never experiment with mains electricity.
    4. At the end of each week, take a short self-test and write down what you still find difficult.
    5. Build a portfolio: save circuit diagrams, code, calculations, project photos and short explanations.

    Suggested free learning tools

    All About Circuits · NPTEL · LTspice · Python · Arduino IDE (later in the course).

    Career direction

    Year 1: fundamentals and study habits. Year 2: core subjects, programming and first internship. Year 3: choose a specialisation such as power systems, power electronics/EVs or automation; build projects and seek internships. Year 4: strong capstone, portfolio, CV, interviews and international applications. These are preparation milestones, not a job guarantee.
